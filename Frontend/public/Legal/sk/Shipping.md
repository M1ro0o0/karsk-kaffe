**Shipping Policy – Karsk Kaffe**

Last updated: 16 March 2026

## 1. Shipping Area

Karsk Kaffe currently ships only within Denmark.

## 2. Shipping Providers

Orders may be delivered using one of the following carriers:

• PostNord
• GLS
• DAO

The available delivery options will be shown during checkout.

## 3. Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days.

Orders placed during weekends or holidays will be processed on the next business day.

## 4. Delivery Time

Delivery times depend on the selected carrier and destination within Denmark. Typical delivery times are between 1–4 business days after shipment.

## 5. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated during checkout based on the selected delivery method.

## 6. Tracking

When available, tracking information will be provided once the order has been shipped.

## 7. Delivery Issues

If you experience problems with a delivery, please contact us:

[info@karskkaffe.dk](mailto:info@karskkaffe.dk)
